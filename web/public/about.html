Planner

A personal daily planner: plan each day, break monthly goals and projects into steps, get a reminder at night to plan tomorrow, and see your Gmail and Outlook inbox in one place so any mail can become a task.

Planner is a private app, run by its owner for themselves and one other person. There is no public sign-up; accounts are created by the owner.

What it does

  • Daily plan: a checklist for each day, with repeating tasks, priorities and reminders.
  • Monthly goals and projects: split into steps that open one at a time, with progress shown.
  • Notifications: a reminder at night to plan tomorrow and a summary of the day in the morning.
  • Mail (optional): connect Gmail or Outlook through Google's or Microsoft's own sign-in to see recent inbox mail, star or flag it, and turn a mail into a task with one tap.

How it uses Google data

When you connect Gmail, Planner reads your inbox from the last 30 days to show it in the app, and changes a mail only when you star or unstar it. It never sends or deletes mail. It stores only the sender, subject, short preview, date and read and starred status; a mail's full text is fetched when you open it and not stored. Google user data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. Details are in the privacy policy.
