Planner privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Planner is a personal daily planner run by its owner for themselves and one other person. It is not offered to the public, and accounts are created only by the owner. This page explains what it stores and how it uses data from Gmail and Outlook when you connect a mailbox.

What Planner stores

  • Your login email address and a hashed password.
  • The tasks, goals, projects and settings you add.
  • For notifications: the push address your browser gives the app.

Gmail and Outlook data

When you connect a mailbox through Google's or Microsoft's own sign-in page, Planner:

  • Reads your inbox from the last 30 days and stores only the sender, subject, short preview, date, read status and whether it is starred or flagged, so the Mail screen opens quickly.
  • Fetches a mail's full text only when you open it, shows it to you, and does not store it.
  • Changes a mail only when you tap the star or flag button in the app.
  • Never sends, forwards or deletes mail.
  • Keeps the sign-in token from Google or Microsoft encrypted on the server. It never sees or stores your Google or Microsoft password.

Planner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Mail data is used only to show your mail to you inside the app and to make tasks from it. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with anyone, and not used to train AI models.

Where it is kept

On one server in Amazon Web Services (Mumbai region), reachable only over HTTPS. Nightly backups are kept for up to 30 days and contain the same data, with sign-in tokens still encrypted.

Removing your data

  • Disconnect a mailbox: Mail → Accounts → Disconnect. Its synced mail and sign-in token are deleted straight away. You can also remove Planner's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or account.live.com/consent/Manage (Microsoft).
  • Mail older than 30 days is removed automatically, except mail you starred.
  • To delete your Planner account and everything in it, ask the owner of this Planner.
